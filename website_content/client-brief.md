Kenya-China Tea Summit 2027 Website Content

**Event Dates:** 21–23 April 2027
Venue: Nairobi, Kenya

# Theme

Brewing Strategic Partnerships for Sustainable Tea Trade, Investment and Innovation

# Homepage Hero Banner

The Premier Tea Trade, Investment & Innovation Forum Connecting Africa and China. Join tea industry leaders, government officials, investors, exporters, importers, researchers, technology providers, and hospitality professionals.

# About the Summit

A landmark international platform designed to deepen trade, investment, innovation, and cultural cooperation between Kenya and China within the tea value chain.

# Why Attend

Meet buyers, exporters, investors, manufacturers, technology providers and policymakers while exploring new market opportunities.

# Summit Objectives

Strengthen trade relations, promote investment, support innovation, facilitate market access, foster collaboration, and promote tea culture.

# Programme

Day 1: Opening Ceremony & Trade Forum. Day 2: Investment Forum, Innovation Conference & B2B Meetings. Day 3: Tea Expo, Cultural Exchange & Closing Ceremony.

# Exhibition

International Tea & Technology Expo featuring tea products, processing equipment, agricultural technologies and logistics services.

Flight booking, hotel reservations, tea plantation tours, safaris and tourism experiences.
